Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through PayPal on our store, we receive your name, shipping address, and purchased item details solely to fulfill and deliver your Print-on-Demand order.

2. Payment Security

We never see, store, or process your credit card or bank details on our servers. All financial transactions are encrypted and handled directly by PayPal.

3. Third-Party Fulfillment Sharing

Your shipping name and delivery address are shared strictly with our Print-on-Demand fulfillment provider and postal carrier to manufacture and ship your package. We never sell, rent, or trade customer information.
