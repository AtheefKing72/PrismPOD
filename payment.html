Payment & Order Processing Policy

1. Real-Time PayPal Checkout

All orders are processed securely in USD via PayPal. You can pay using your PayPal balance, debit card, or major credit card, backed by PayPal Buyer Protection.

2. Order Verification & Printing Timeline

Once your payment is confirmed, our team manually reviews your order details and submits your design to our Print-on-Demand production facility within 24 hours.Shipping takes 1-3 businesdays.

3. Returns & Replacements

Because each item is custom-printed specifically for your order, we cannot accept returns for buyer's remorse. However, if an item arrives damaged or with a printing defect, we will issue a free replacement or full refund.
